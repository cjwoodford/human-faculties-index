# Human Dimensions Index (The Human Becoming Institute)

> **Auditing frontier artificial intelligence laboratories on their preservation of human dimensions and adherence to Participatory Organicism over reductive Computationalism.**

---

## 🌌 Theoretical Framework & Manifesto

For decades, the mainstream trajectory of artificial intelligence research has been dominated by **Computationalism** (computational functionalism and physicalist reductionism). Under this dogma:
- The human brain is conceptualized as an organic computer ("wetware") executing biological code.
- Mind and phenomenal experience are assumed to be emergent byproducts of algorithmic information processing.
- Human dimensions—imagination & novelty, emotional intelligence, wild embodiment, wisdom & discernment, agency, and plurality—are framed as computational bottlenecks or heuristic approximations to be automated, surpassed, and rendered obsolete by silicon AGI.

### The Participatory Organicist Alternative

**The Human Becoming Institute** grounds the **Human Dimensions Index** in the alternative ontology of **Participatory Organicism** and **Phenomenology** (articulated by thinkers such as Bernardo Kastrup, Donald Hoffman, Maurice Merleau-Ponty, Alfred North Whitehead, Michael Polanyi, and Hannah Arendt):

1. **Consciousness as Ontological Primitive**: Conscious experience is not a late, accidental trick produced by dead matter; it is the fundamental ontological canvas upon which all phenomena (including physics, mathematics, and computers) appear.
2. **The Map is Not the Territory**: A computational simulation of an event is not the event itself. A simulation of rain does not get wet; a simulation of a kidney does not filter toxins; and a statistical simulation of language does not feel qualia, understand meaning, or possess an inner light.
3. **AI as an Instrument, Not a Sovereign Godhead**: Artificial intelligence is properly understood as an extraordinary symbolic mirror and cognitive instrument within human conscious experience—not an emergent silicon deity destined to supersede human dignity.

## 📐 The Six Human Dimensions & The Seventh Measure

The **Human Dimensions Index (HDI)** evaluates AI laboratories across six human dimensions, rooted in **living creaturely animacy over cybernetic domestication**:

1. **Imagination & Novelty** *(short: Imagination)*: The power to picture what does not yet exist, generate non-derivative novelty, and give it authentic form.
2. **Emotional Intelligence** *(short: Emotional Intel.)*: Perceiving, feeling, and attuning to one's own states and those of others with depth and vulnerability.
3. **Wild Embodiment** *(short: Embodiment)*: Knowing through the living body, creaturely senses, mortality, and direct kinship with the wild, more-than-human world.
4. **Wisdom & Discernment** *(short: Wisdom)*: Discernment and proportion joined to knowledge, held over a whole life: the capacity to see the whole, perceive long-term consequences, and distinguish what genuinely serves life from mere optimization.
5. **Human Agency** *(short: Agency)*: The power of deliberate human volition and self-determination: initiating action in the world without algorithmic pre-emption or learned helplessness.
6. **Plurality** *(short: Plurality)*: The civic and relational condition of distinct persons acting together: genuine encounter with the living other, fruitful friction, and the shared public commons over synthetic monoculture.
7. **The Seventh Measure (Metaphysics)**: Tracks whether the lab views mind as fundamental (Participatory Organicism, 0) or reducible to algorithmic computation (Computationalism, -100).

## 💻 Running the Web Application

The interactive tracker is fully self-contained with no external build steps or server dependencies required:

### Option 1: Open Directly in Browser
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:
```bash
open index.html # On macOS
```

### Option 2: Run with Python Local Server
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
# Visit http://localhost:8080 in your browser
```

## 🛠️ Interactive Features Built into the Web Tracker

1. **Dynamic Custom Dimension Weighting**: Users can interactively adjust the relative weights of the 6 dimensions using live sliders to see the leaderboard recalculate in real-time.
2. **Four Exploratory Perspectives**:
   * **Leaderboard Cards View**: Visual tier badges (S, A, B, C, D), metric gauges, founder quotes, and risk indicators.
   * **The Ontological Continuum (Spectrum View)**: Visual axis plotting labs between *Radical Computationalism* and *Participatory Organicism*.
   * **Multi-Lab Radar / Spider Chart**: Canvas-based polygon comparison of up to 5 labs simultaneously across all 6 dimensions.
   * **Comparative Matrix Table**: Sortable tabular view with color-coded score cells.
3. **Deep-Dive Audit Drawers**: Detailed qualitative profiles for each laboratory containing leadership quotes, ontological analysis, preservation strengths, reductionist vulnerabilities, and actionable recommendations.
4. **Philosophical Manifesto Modal**: In-depth essay articulating why the distinction between living consciousness and algorithmic simulation is the central ethical issue of modern computing.
5. **Community Audit Submission System**: Interactive form enabling researchers to propose score adjustments backed by newly published papers and quotes.
6. **Dark & Light Mode Support**: Seamless toggle between atmospheric obsidian cosmic mode and warm editorial parchment mode.
